ABOUT ME

Mohamed Moez Oueslati

I am a marketing and communications specialist based in Tunis. For the past two years, I've been telling the story of Tunisia's impact ecosystem at Impact Partner, turning calls for applications, workshops and events into campaigns that move people to engage, participate and take action.

HOW I THINK

I love the mix of strategy, creativity and a bit of growth hacking. From building campaigns from scratch to finding clever ways to get more people involved, I enjoy the whole process: creating, experimenting, distributing and measuring what works.

Over the past two years, that's meant turning event insights into content, making participants brand ambassadors through personalized visuals, and getting 50 applications with less than $20 in ad spend.

I'm at my best when communication goes beyond visibility: when it gets people talking, sharing and taking action.

The challenge

GrowImpact supports Tunisian impact startups through acceleration, expert support, and access to funding. Each phase depends on the right people seeing the right message at the right time. The goal was to reach beyond Impact Partner's existing followers and turn calls for applications into real engagement, in a crowded space full of competing opportunities.

My approach

In 2026, the Matching Fund campaign paired a clear call for applications with a "Meet the Jury" series to build credibility, reaching over 10,000 accounts and bringing in 339 new followers organically.

The challenge

Station I brings investors, corporates, support organizations, and impact entrepreneurs together around one question: how to move capital toward impact. Most editions were invitation-only, held in small rooms with a selected audience. The challenge was to give these closed-door conversations a public life and build Station I into a recognizable brand across four editions, four venues, and changing formats, from a Slush'D side event in Djerba to CSR evenings, masterclasses, and fireside chats in Tunis.

My approach

I built a repeatable campaign for this initiative: a branded announcement, speaker and quote cards, then recaps through photo carousels and short videos, and two white papers capturing the key outputs. Every visual carried the same Station I identity, adapted to each partner and venue. On LinkedIn, recap carousels drove 734 of 893 clicks and a 44.3% engagement rate on the investment workshop. Facebook carried reach with 9.2K accounts, while Instagram generated the most interaction. The CSR Meets Impact edition also launched Impact Partner's 10 Years of Impact report, extending the conversation well beyond the room.

Impact Report

November 2025

The challenge

Helping ten years of programs, data and founder stories become one clear, credible document that donors, partners and entrepreneurs actually read.

My role

Designing the 64-page layout and data visuals and structuring the content across ten chapters, while the MEAL Manager led the data collection and analysis. I also led the launch communication at Station I.

The objective

Show a decade of measurable impact, strengthen trust with donors and partners, and set the agenda for Impact Partner's next ten years.

Results & impact

64pages designed and laid out
10chapters and 10 founder stories
339beneficiaries surveyed behind the data
1.6Kdocument clicks on LinkedIn at launch

Key findings the report brought to life

215+startups accelerated since 2014
2,500jobs created and sustained
67%of supported startups led by women
25.99MTND in investment mobilized

Our approach

In collaboration with Impact Partner's MEAL Manager, who led the data work, I designed the layout and structured ten years of program data, survey results and interviews into ten chapters, from methodology to gender inclusion and financial impact, then gave every key number its own data visual so readers can grasp the impact in minutes. Ten founder stories put faces to the figures. The report launched at Station I's CSR Meets Impact edition and drove 1.6K document clicks on LinkedIn.
